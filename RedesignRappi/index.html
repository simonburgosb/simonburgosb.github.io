PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding the Problem

The Challenge

Rappi provides convenience through its super-app ecosystem, but users frequently experience frustration caused by delivery uncertainty, support inefficiencies, and limited transparency throughout the ordering process.

Goal

Identify the main friction points in the customer journey and redesign critical touchpoints to increase trust, transparency and retention.

My Role

UX Researcher, Service Designer and Product Designer responsible for research synthesis, journey mapping, service blueprinting and interface redesign.

Methodologies

Secondary Research, Atomic Research, Journey Mapping, Service Blueprint, How Might We, Ideation and High-Fidelity Prototyping.

KEY INSIGHTS

What We Learned

Transparency Builds Trust

Users feel significantly more confident when delivery information, pricing details and support processes are visible and understandable.

Uncertainty Creates Friction

Delays are tolerated when explained. Lack of information is what generates frustration.

Support Influences Loyalty

Support interactions often determine whether users stay or abandon the platform.

Personalization Reduces Cognitive Load

Most users only use a small subset of available services, making personalization a major opportunity.

ATOMIC RESEARCH

Evidence-Based Findings

Fact

Users frequently report uncertainty during delivery.

Insight

The issue is not the delay itself but the lack of information.

Recommendation

Provide predictive updates and proactive communication.

Fact

Users struggle to understand promotions and discounts.

Insight

Confusing rewards reduce perceived value.

Recommendation

Create transparent savings indicators.

USER JOURNEY

Understanding User Emotions

01 Awareness

Users open Rappi looking for a quick solution to satisfy an immediate need.

02 Exploration

Users compare restaurants, promotions and delivery times.

03 Checkout

Users expect transparency and confidence before paying.

04 Delivery

The lowest emotional point due to uncertainty and lack of communication.

05 Support

Critical moment for recovering trust and reducing frustration.

06 Loyalty

Positive experiences encourage repeated use and retention.

SERVICE BLUEPRINT

Identifying System Opportunities

Frontstage Opportunities

  • Improve pricing transparency.
  • Provide clearer delivery communication.
  • Reduce checkout uncertainty.
  • Increase status visibility.

Backstage Opportunities

  • Improve ETA prediction systems.
  • Automate order verification.
  • Reduce escalation delays.
  • Improve operational visibility.

Support Opportunities

  • Faster human support access.
  • Visible ticket status.
  • Recovery-focused experiences.
  • More empathetic communication.
HOW MIGHT WE

How might we reduce uncertainty and increase trust throughout the ordering experience by improving transparency, personalization and support visibility?

DESIGN SOLUTIONS

Proposed Experience Improvements

01

Smart Tracking

Real-time predictive notifications and improved ETA accuracy to reduce uncertainty.

02

Visual Order Verification

Photo confirmation before delivery completion to reduce missing-item complaints.

03

Personalized Homepage

Adaptive navigation and recommendations based on user behavior.

04

Human-Centered Support

Faster escalation paths and more transparent ticket management.
